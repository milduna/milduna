Privacy Policy

Milduna — vocabulary learning app for Android, iOS and the web.
Version 2.0 — Last updated: August 24, 2026

In short: Milduna works without an account — in guest mode your data never leaves your device. If you create an account, we store your learning progress in the cloud (Firebase) so you find it again on every device. The App is free and shows advertising (Google AdMob); it sells nothing. Pronunciation audio is streamed from Cloudflare. We never sell your data, and we never send your learning progress to advertisers.

1. Publisher and data controller

Milduna — published by Amaury PASCHER.
Contact for any privacy question: contact@milduna.com

The full publisher identity (legal form, registered address, registration number) is set out in the Legal Notice.

No Data Protection Officer has been appointed: the processing described below does not meet the conditions of Article 37 GDPR.

2. What we collect, and when

2.1 Guest mode — no account, nothing leaves your device

You can use the whole app without signing in. Your progress (words learned, streaks, unlocked lessons, keys, display preferences, daily goal) is written only to the local storage of your browser or device (localStorage). This data is never sent to our servers. It is lost if you uninstall the app or clear your browser data, and it cannot be recovered.

2.2 Creating an account — Firebase Authentication (Google LLC)

If you choose to save your progress, we use Firebase Authentication to sign you in. What is shared depends on the method you pick:

Sign-in methodData we receive
Google Sign-InEmail address, display name and profile picture attached to your Google account — provided by Google, with your explicit authorisation at sign-in.
Sign in with Apple iOSYour name (if you agree to share it) and an email address. If you choose Hide My Email, Apple gives us only a relay address ending in @privaterelay.appleid.com — we never see your real address.
Email / passwordYour email address. The password is created, hashed and stored by Firebase — we never have access to it in clear text, and we cannot read it.

A unique account identifier (Firebase UID) is generated. It is the only key linking you to the data described below — it contains no name, no email and no device identifier.

2.3 Your progress — Cloud Firestore (Google LLC)

Once signed in, your progress is synced to Cloud Firestore in a single document keyed to your UID (users/{uid}). It holds: words and lessons completed, streak and daily-goal statistics, unlocked lessons and themes, keys earned, the language pairs you study, and display preferences.

It does not hold your name, your email address, your location, your contacts, or anything you type outside the exercises. Firestore security rules restrict every read and write to the signed-in owner of the document: no other user, signed in or not, can reach it.

2.4 Invitation codes

If you share an invitation code, we store two minimal records: the code and the account identifier that owns it, and, for each person who enters that code, the fact that they did and when. The code itself is derived from the account identifier through a SHA-256 hash and cannot be reversed back to it.

No name, email address or learning progress passes through these records. They exist only so a referral reward can be granted, and they are erased when the account is deleted.

2.5 Advertising — Google AdMob

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. Depending on your region and your choices, AdMob may collect:

  • a resettable advertising identifier — the AAID on Android, the IDFA on iOS;
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, whether a rewarded ad was watched through);
  • general technical information: device type, operating system, approximate country.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows a Google-certified consent screen (CMP) on first launch, before any ad is requested. If you decline, ads may still appear but are non-personalised. You can change your answer at any time from Settings → Ad preferences inside the app.

On iOS, personalised advertising also requires your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline it, the IDFA is not accessible to AdMob and advertising stays non-personalised.

AdMob has no access to your Milduna account or your learning progress. See the Advertising & Consent page for details.

2.6 Pronunciation audio — Cloudflare, Inc.

Pronunciation clips are pre-generated files stored in Cloudflare R2 and delivered by a Cloudflare Worker. Playing a word sends a request to Cloudflare, which — like any web server — receives your IP address and standard technical request data (date, user agent, file requested) and keeps them for a limited period for security, anti-abuse and delivery purposes.

No account identifier, no cookie and no progress data is attached to these requests: they cannot be tied back to your account.

2.7 Hosting of the web version and of these documents

The web version of the app is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, and the legal documents you are reading on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Both receive your IP address and standard technical request data when a page loads, and keep them for a limited period for security purposes. Neither places advertising or analytics cookies for us — we run no audience-measurement or analytics tool, on the web or in the app.

2.8 Reminders

If you enable them, study reminders are scheduled and displayed by your device itself. No message passes through a server, and we hold no notification token. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your system settings.

2.9 Technical and diagnostic data

Like any app built on Google, Apple and Firebase SDKs, standard technical data may be collected automatically for operation and diagnostics: app version, language, device type, crash reports. This is handled by those providers under their own privacy policies.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and progress: for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account erases them immediately (see section 6).
  • Guest data: stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app — we hold no copy.
  • Technical server logs (Cloudflare, GitHub, Google): short retention periods set by each provider, for security purposes.

6. Your rights

If you are in the European Union — or in a country granting equivalent rights — you may exercise the following over your data:

  • Access: obtain a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Erasure: delete your account and everything attached to it — immediately and without asking us, from Settings → Delete my account in the app, or from the account deletion page, which needs no installation.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Restriction and objection: including objecting to processing based on our legitimate interest.
  • Withdrawal of consent: at any time, in particular for personalised advertising, from Settings → Ad preferences. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.

To exercise a right other than self-service deletion, write to contact@milduna.com. We answer within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

7. Children

Milduna is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age applicable in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data without appropriate parental consent. If you believe a child has given us data without such consent, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All traffic between the app and our services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your Firestore document is restricted by security rules to your authenticated account alone. Passwords are never stored by us: Firebase handles them, hashed.

No system is invulnerable. Should a personal data breach be likely to result in a high risk to you, we will notify you and the CNIL as required by Articles 33 and 34 GDPR.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the service or the law evolves. The version number and date at the top of the page always reflect the current text. If a change materially affects your rights or the way your data is used, we will tell you inside the app before it takes effect.
